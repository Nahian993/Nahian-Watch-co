    CROWN WATCH CO. is a Bangladesh-based watch and calculator business with approximately 50 years of heritage, selling watches, smartwatches,
  calculators, accessories, and professional watch repair services.

    Core brand philosophy:
    “50 Years of Timekeeping Heritage, Precision, and Trust.”

    - Professional after-sales service

    Initial market:
    - Bangladesh only

    - Online delivery throughout Bangladesh
    - Primary languages: Bangla and English
    - Primary currency: Bangladeshi Taka, BDT

    Target customers:
    - Students
    - Parents
    - Children
    - Corporate professionals
    - Men and women
    - Couples
    - Wedding shoppers
    - Gift buyers
    - Watch enthusiasts
    - Collectors
    - Accountants and business owners
    - Customers needing watch repair

    - Affordable watches
    - Smartwatches
    - Casio, Citizen, Seiko, and other genuine brands where authenticity can be verified
    - Limited luxury and mechanical watches
    - Children’s watches
    - Men’s and women’s watches

    - Calculators

    ==================================================
    PART V: PERSONALIZATION RULES
    ==================================================

    Design a responsible personalization system.

    Do not infer income, wealth, identity, or purchasing power from a visitor’s phone model, browser, IP address, or device. Do not show expensive products
   merely because someone uses an expensive device.

    Use transparent and consent-aware signals such as:
    - Categories viewed
    - Price filters selected
    - Search terms
    - Previous purchases
    - Wishlist activity
    - Declared preferences
    - Occasion selected
    - Gift recipient type
    - Style preferences
    - Budget selected voluntarily
    - Location only when necessary for delivery

    Allow customers to choose:
    - Budget
    - Style
    - Brand
    - Gender preference if desired
    - Occasion
    - Watch type
    - Features
    - Delivery location

    Explain personalization clearly and provide controls to disable it.
